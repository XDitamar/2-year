# Photo Upload Guide for Anniversary Netflix Clone

## Folder Structure Created

I have created the following directories in the `public` folder for your anniversary photos:

### 📁 public/6-months/
Upload 10 photos from your first 6 months together
- Name them: `1 (1).jpeg`, `1 (2).jpeg`, `1 (3).jpeg`, `1 (4).jpeg`, `1 (5).jpeg`, `1 (6).jpeg`, `1 (7).jpeg`, `1 (8).jpeg`, `1 (9).jpeg`, `1 (10).jpeg`

### 📁 public/1-year/
Upload 10 photos from your first year together
- Name them: `1 (1).jpeg`, `1 (2).jpeg`, `1 (3).jpeg`, `1 (4).jpeg`, `1 (5).jpeg`, `1 (6).jpeg`, `1 (7).jpeg`, `1 (8).jpeg`, `1 (9).jpeg`, `1 (10).jpeg`

### 📁 public/2-years/
Upload 10 photos from your 2-year journey together
- Name them: `1 (1).jpeg`, `1 (2).jpeg`, `1 (3).jpeg`, `1 (4).jpeg`, `1 (5).jpeg`, `1 (6).jpeg`, `1 (7).jpeg`, `1 (8).jpeg`, `1 (9).jpeg`, `1 (10).jpeg`

## Additional Media Files Needed

### 📁 public/profile/
- `profile.jpeg` - Alina's profile picture

### 📁 public/intro/
- `intro.mp4` - Your custom intro video

### 📁 public/hero/
- `bg.jpg` - Background image for the hero section

### 📁 public/
- `anniversary-video.mp4` - Main anniversary video that plays in the video player

## Notes
- All photos should be in JPG format
- Videos should be in MP4 format
- The application will automatically display these photos in the content rows on the home screen
- Each anniversary period (6 months, 1 year, 2 years) will show as a separate row with 10 photos each
